🛒 Supermarket Case Study


📋 Overview


This project involves designing interactive Power BI dashboards to analyze a dataset of 5,800+ transactions, uncovering valuable insights into sales trends, profitability, customer segmentation, and shipping performance. The dashboards provide actionable recommendations to improve business outcomes.

🌟 Features
📈 Sales Analysis: Track revenue trends and profitability across regions and categories.
👥 Customer Insights: Analyze customer segmentation and regional performance.
🚚 Shipping Performance: Assess delivery trends and operational efficiency.
🎨 Interactive Visualizations: Drill-down filters for deeper exploration of data.
📊 Key Insights
🌍 The West region accounted for 40% of customers but had 20% lower profit margins compared to the East, enabling targeted strategies to boost profitability.
💸 Identified 15% higher profitability in specific sub-categories, driving a focus on high-margin products.
📦 Highlighted key shipping inefficiencies, allowing for recommendations to improve delivery times and customer satisfaction.
🛠️ Tools and Technologies
Power BI: For designing dashboards and visualizations.
Data Transformation: Conducted using Power Query.
Data Sources: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/yesshivam007/superstore-dataset
📂 Dashboards and Visualizations
💵 Sales Overview: Monthly sales trends, category profitability, and top products.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Insights: Regional segmentation and retention analysis.
📊 Shipping Performance: Metrics for delivery efficiency and costs.
